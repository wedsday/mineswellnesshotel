Are you tired of living at the same place for so long? Would you want to live in a whole new different environment where there are traditions and culture that may surprise you? And where the people accept diversity. Well don’t worry because the place where you will enjoy is Malaysia. You can be a Malaysian Expat and if you don’t know what that means, a Malaysian Expat is a foreigner who migrates and lives in Malaysia. You might be wondering, what are the things you can get when becoming [an expatriate in Malaysia](http://www.klexpatmalaysia.com/)?

Being a Malaysian Expat allows you to be able to experience a different culture and traditional than the one you experience in your home. Don’t worry about the people in Malaysia because there nice and easily accepts diversity. Malaysia also has wonderful views that may not be common in your home town and the props that they will use for their festivals will be all new to you. If you had a hard time using your public transportation, you wouldn’t have to worry about that when you are in Malaysia.

 

In Malaysia there is a lot of public transportation that can help you go from point A to point B. If you also enjoy eating exotic food then Malaysia is the place for you. Malaysia has exotic food that can drive your taste buds into happiness. Don’t worry if you might not like it at first because there is international food in Malaysia, so don’t worry about going hungry because you won’t. In Malaysia though they have their own language but the people can speak and understand English, so if you start being a [Malaysian Expat](https://www.internations.org/malaysia-expats), language barrier wouldn’t be much of a problem for you, but if you would want to learn their language then that’s awesome too.

 

In Malaysia, specifically Kuala Lumpur, due to modernization, its building is design to be beautiful, and at the same time, functional. You can get any condo with room up to three people to six people and each one have big glass so that when you wake up you get to see a wonderful view. You would also enjoy the beautiful beaches of Malaysia so if you are the one to enjoy a good swim in the beach then Malaysia is your new home. Their beaches are very beautiful and clean and there some beaches that haven’t been touch by buildings, so it would mean that when you start swimming, it is only you, the sea and the environment.

 

Being a Malaysian Expat sure has some amazing benefits to it because you get to enjoy a whole new different culture and tradition and at the same time the people in Malaysia are nice, hospitable and they don’t think much of cultural difference or rather they accept diversity. So when you try to be a Malaysian Expat, you bet you can enjoy it, especially since there are some parts of Malaysia that have so many trees and beaches.
