1. Activities- You can watch elephants at the sanctuary, and you'll love having the chance to view them and see them interact with one another. Not only that, but you might be given permission to feed the elephants and in the past you were sometimes allowed to ride them, but you may not be able to do it. However, the sanctuary allows you to get a glimpse of elephants, so if you've never had the chance to see an elephant in person, then you'll want to visit the sanctuary.</p>

<p>Also, you'll be able to watch the elephants getting fed and bathed. You and your family will enjoy this experience because there are not many places in the country that allow you to do this. If you want to see elephants up-close, then you'll want to check out this sanctuary.</p>
