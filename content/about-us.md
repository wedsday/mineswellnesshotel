Welcome to Mine's Wellness — a blog for explorers and for travellers who still want a little advice before they go. We are a group of friends who share the same interest: travel, rediscover familiar places, and write about what actually helps on the ground.

We travel together or on our own, so you can expect articles that mix experience with practical tips — more useful than a brochure, and more personal than a booking site.

**What we publish**

- Destination notes under [Place](/category/place/)
- Practical advice under [Travel Tips](/category/travel-tips/)
- Answers to common questions on the [FAQ](/faq/)

We do **not** sell hotel packages or process bookings here. If you want to get in touch, visit [Contact Us](/contact-us/).
