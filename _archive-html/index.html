Great Italian comfort food Kuala Lumpur

2017-04-23 By Admin Leave a Comment

We found a great new hot spot in Kuala Lumpur. The best part about this place is it serves Italian food all day long. For anyone who is a big lover of Italian cuisine in Malaysia, it is agreed that it can be a challenge to find a restaurant that is great but also offers a good setting to relax and have fun while enjoying authentic Italian meals. At Sassorosso Italian restaurant, this is possible.

We found this restaurant a few blocks from our office. It is in Lorong Yap Kwan Seng and it looks really pretty as you walk up to it. You can almost smell the pizza as it is baking. When we paid our visit after work we were delighted to find that it doubles as a sports bar. It looks like a romantic restaurant with elegant table settings and dim lighting. But inside there is an actual sports bar where people like us, tourists and ex-patriots hang out to watch TV and enjoy drinks.

Here is a recap of the restaurant starting with the food:

The website does not lie when it says it offers authentic Italian food. This is real Italian food that will please your taste buds. All real lovers of this cuisine know what authentic cuisine should taste like. This restaurant did not fail in this area.

We love the pizza and there are many different types of cheeses to choose from along with that mouth watering thin crust. If you pass by Sassorosso at lunch time be sure to try one a
bout à la carte meals. Make sure to try the seafood.

The Service

Expect truly high-end service when you visit this restaurant. Before you arrive be sure to look online to know the dress code. Sassorosso does have a dress code and it is out of consideration for all the diners. You’ll notice that the service crew looks elegant. Each service member also knows the menu very well.

You know from the way they tell you about the specials for the day that they are professionally trained to know exactly what goes into every meal. This adds to the authentic Italian feel at Sassorosso. We even got to meet the chef on one of our visits he was warm and congenial.

As for being a sports bar, it differs from many of the sports bars in Malaysia because it also has an Italian restaurant as part of it. This is what makes this place so refreshing. We took the time to enjoy hanging out at the sports bar. It’s very chill.

It’s perfect for enjoying drinks and watching TV after a long day at work. It isn’t as dark or as loud as a normal sports bar, but you get great drinks, HDTV, and updates on all your favorite sports.

The next time that you are in Kuala Lumpur, or you live here and just want to visit a great sports bar and enjoy some tasty Italian food be sure to visit Sassorosso. Also, make sure you always check their website ahead of time to find out the dress code and the meal specials.

You can find all this information out at their website which is sassoross.com.my. Take our advice and be sure to visit one of the best Italian restaurant in KL.

Information About Kuala Gandah Elephant Sanctuary

2016-09-19 By Admin Leave a Comment

The most popular elephant sanctuary in Malaysia is the Kuala Gandah Elephant Sanctuary. If you have never been there, then you should consider going. Read on for more information about this sanctuary, such as activities you can do there, why you should go and other useful info.

1. Activities- You can watch elephants at the sanctuary, and you’ll love having the chance to view them and see them interact with one another. Not only that, but you might be given permission to feed the elephants and in the past you were sometimes allowed to ride them, but you may not be able to do it. However, the sanctuary allows you to get a glimpse of elephants, so if you’ve never had the chance to see an elephant in person, then you’ll want to visit the sanctuary.

Also, you’ll be able to watch the elephants getting fed and bathed. You and your family will enjoy this experience because there are not many places in the country that allow you to do this. If you want to see elephants up-close, then you’ll want to check out this sanctuary.

2. Who Should Go- If you are a fan of elephants, then you should go to Kuala Gandah Elephant Sanctuary. You can easily spend an hour or more there, and this is because time will fly by when you watch the elephants. If you want to find out how elephants act around other elephants, then this is the place to go.

People who have families should go, especially families with children. Kids love elephants and if you bring them to the sanctuary, then it will be an experience they will not soon forget. Your kids will love watching elephants interact with one another and they will love watching them get bathed and fed.

Those who are in Malaysia on vacation should also go to the sanctuary. It will give them something unique and fun to do, and it will give them the chance to learn about elephants. It doesn’t matter who you are or whether you’re in Malaysia on vacation on your own or with your family, you will want to make sure you visit the sanctuary because it is one of the top attractions in the country.

3. Buying Tickets- You don’t have to purchase any tickets. This is because entry into the sanctuary is completely free. All you have to do is arrange for transport and then you’ll be able to enter the sanctuary at no charge. This is another reason why you should visit the Kuala Gandah Elephant Sanctuary, and it doesn’t matter where in Malaysia you are staying, it is worth making the trip to Kuala Gandah.

As you can see, you should pay a visit Kuala Gandah Elephant Sanctuary, especially since it doesn’t cost money to enter. All you have to do now is book your trip to Malaysia and make your way to the sanctuary. The sooner you book your trip, the sooner you can see elephants in person.

Getting Around Scenic Penang – The Car Rental Option

2016-09-08 By Admin Leave a Comment

Penang Malayisa is one of the most popular weekend destinations in Southeast Asia, in part due to its proximity to Singapore (it’s only about 1.5 hours by plane and there are numerous daily flights), the source of much of the tourism to this tiny Malaysian island.

The attractions of Penang are almost too numerous to mention, however near the top of the list must be the historic attractions of old Georgetown and the lovely beaches that can be found across the island. The exceptional seafood restaurants and excellent markets, five star international hotels as well as some stunning cultural attractions round out some of the best that Penang has to offer.

One of the easiest ways of getting under the skin of this great travel destination is to hire a a car in Penang. It’s also one of the most cost effective if you are traveling with family or a group of friends. 24 hour car hire from a reputable and well known car hire compay will cost in the region of RM 150 to RM 500 (about USD $35 – USD $120) per day. However, savvy travellers will know that there are numerous smaller car rental Penang agencies in places like Georgetown where you can get a significantly better deal than that if you’re willing to lower your expectations as far as getting the latest models are concerned. Some of the options are a bit lower down the scale – but perfect if you just want to have a day out on the island.

Before you take the plunge and hire a car in Penang be aware that it takes nerves of steal to negotiate some of the smaller roadways on the island. Georgetown (the Old Town) is especially challenging. That said if you’re used to traffic in some of the larger cities in the world and know how to assert yourself without being rude you’ll be fine.

Due to the fact that road signage on Penang can offer some challenges to the foreigner we visitor it is great idea to rent a GPS system from the car rental agency that you are using. You could use the data on your phone, but if you’re on an international phone which doesn’t specifically cater to Malaysian use beware – your charges can go through the roof – a GPS is by far a better choice.

For some people who want to use Penang as a jump off point for exploration of the rest of Malaysia it’s preferable to use one of the larger car rental agencies, the smaller independent ones sometimes don’t allow you to leave the sialnd with their car. However, if you choose a larger car rental agency they sometimes offer drop off points throughout Malaysia whcih makes Penang a great choice for starting off your Malaysian adventure.

Penang is fabulous for those who want to dip their toes into the Southeast Asian experience. It’s the perfect mix between the old and the new, so book your trip right now.
